# Tailoring and factual integrity rules

1. Treat the experience master as a candidate source, not unquestionable truth: check every claim against its cited evidence.
2. Never change dates, job level, ownership, scope, metrics or qualifications to satisfy a vacancy.
3. Distinguish direct work from involvement, co-leadership, observation, research proposals and future plans.
4. Do not claim a research idea or ongoing project is a demonstrated investable result.
5. Preserve historical CVs unchanged; append new versions instead of overwriting.
6. Tailor evidence selection and emphasis to the vacancy, not the facts.
7. Use precise action/method/outcome bullet structure; prefer real results over generic adjectives.
8. Keep privacy and confidentiality constraints; do not publish internal employer details unless cleared.
9. Before submission, check the employer, title, dates, metrics, active PDF content, and role-specific factual fit.
10. Save the submitted CV version and job description together, and log the outcome separately.
